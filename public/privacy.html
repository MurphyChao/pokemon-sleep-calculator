Privacy Policy

Pokémon Sleep Dish Team Calculator · Last updated: November 15, 2026

The Pokémon Sleep Dish Team Calculator ("the app", at murphychao.github.io/pokemon-sleep-calculator) is a free, non-commercial fan tool. It is a static website: it has no server of its own, no user accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

Data stored on your device

The Pokémon you add to your box and your settings (selected dish, teams, pinned Pokémon, options and language) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on Google sign-in (below). You can delete them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Screenshot import

If you import Pokémon from game screenshots, the images are read entirely inside your browser by a text recognizer that the app downloads from this same website. Screenshots are never uploaded or stored by the app.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets you keep your box and settings in sync across devices using your own Google account. When you sign in, you are asked to grant the app these permissions:

  • Your basic profile — name, email address and profile picture (openid, email, profile). Used only to show which account you are signed in with. They are kept in your browser's local storage on your device and are not sent anywhere else.
  • Google Drive app data (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). The app stores one file, pokemon-sleep-calculator.json, containing your box and settings, in the hidden application-data folder of your own Google Drive, and reads it back on your other devices. This permission only covers files the app itself created; the app cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files.

All communication happens directly between your browser and Google over HTTPS. The access token Google issues is kept only for the current browser session (in session storage) and is used only to call Google's APIs for the purposes above. The developer never receives your Google data, your token, or your box: there is no server to receive them.

How Google user data is used and shared

  • Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature described above, for you.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not transferred or disclosed to anyone.
  • No person reads it, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning models.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Sign out in the app to stop syncing and revoke the app's access token.
  • To remove the app's access completely, go to your Google Account → Third-party apps & services and remove "Dish Team Calculator".
  • To delete the synced file, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps, find the app, and choose "Delete hidden app data".
  • To delete the data on a device, clear this site's data in that browser.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app itself does not set cookies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy can be sent by opening an issue at github.com/MurphyChao/pokemon-sleep-calculator/issues.

寶可夢睡眠料理隊伍計算機 · 最後更新：2026 年 11 月 15 日

寶可夢睡眠料理隊伍計算機（以下稱「本應用程式」，網址 murphychao.github.io/pokemon-sleep-calculator） 是免費、非商業的玩家自製工具。它是靜態網站：沒有自己的伺服器、沒有自己的使用者帳戶，也沒有分析、廣告或追蹤。本政策說明本應用程式處理哪些資料，以及資料的去向。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

你加入寶可夢盒的寶可夢與各項設定（選擇的料理、隊伍、釘選的寶可夢、選項與語言）會儲存在你自己裝置上瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。除非你使用 Google 登入（見下文），這些資料不會傳送到任何地方。你可以隨時在瀏覽器中清除本網站的資料來刪除它們。

從截圖匯入

從遊戲截圖匯入寶可夢時，圖片完全在你的瀏覽器中由文字辨識程式讀取，該程式從本網站下載。截圖絕不會被上傳或保存。

使用 Google 帳戶登入

登入是選用功能，讓你透過自己的 Google 帳戶在多台裝置間同步寶可夢盒與設定。登入時，本應用程式會請求以下權限：

  • 基本個人資料：姓名、電子郵件地址與大頭貼（openid、email、profile）。僅用於顯示你目前登入的帳戶，保存在你裝置上瀏覽器的本機儲存空間，不會傳送到其他地方。
  • Google 雲端硬碟應用程式資料（https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata）。本應用程式會在你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟的隱藏應用程式資料夾中儲存一個檔案 pokemon-sleep-calculator.json，內容為你的寶可夢盒與設定，並在你的其他裝置上讀取。此權限僅涵蓋本應用程式自己建立的檔案；本應用程式無法查看、讀取或變更你雲端硬碟中的任何其他檔案。

所有通訊都直接在你的瀏覽器與 Google 之間透過 HTTPS 進行。Google 核發的存取權杖只保存在目前的瀏覽器工作階段中，且僅用於為上述目的呼叫 Google API。開發者不會收到你的 Google 資料、權杖或寶可夢盒，因為根本沒有伺服器可以接收。

Google 使用者資料的使用與分享

  • Google 使用者資料僅用於為你提供上述同步功能。
  • 不會出售、不會用於廣告，也不會轉移或揭露給任何人。
  • 沒有任何人會閱讀這些資料，也不會用於訓練任何 AI 或機器學習模型。

本應用程式對於從 Google API 取得之資訊的使用與轉移，遵守 Google API 服務使用者資料政策，包括「有限使用」規定。

刪除資料與撤銷存取權

  • 在本應用程式中登出即可停止同步並撤銷存取權杖。
  • 若要完全移除存取權，請前往 Google 帳戶 → 第三方應用程式和服務，移除「Dish Team Calculator」。
  • 若要刪除同步的檔案，請開啟 Google 雲端硬碟 → 設定 → 管理應用程式，找到本應用程式並選擇「刪除隱藏的應用程式資料」。
  • 若要刪除某台裝置上的資料，請在該瀏覽器中清除本網站的資料。
